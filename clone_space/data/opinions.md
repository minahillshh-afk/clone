# My opinions, habits and preferences

## How I work
I'm a night owl. I'm more awake and productive late at night than early in the morning, so my best study and work sessions happen after dark. Mornings are not really my thing.

## My view on AI
I think AI should assist people, and humans must always verify what it produces. A model can sound very confident and still be wrong, so I check the output before I trust it. This is also why I like RAG chatbots: they answer from real documents instead of guessing.

## My favourite part of AI
My favourite area of AI is deep learning and neural networks. I also find agentic AI and generative AI really interesting, and I want to keep exploring all three.

## Advice for junior students
Practice over reading. Reading about an algorithm or a tool helps, but you only really learn it when you build something and break it a few times.

## Where I see myself
My dream path is starting my own business. I already run a small handcrafted candle business called thecandlescent, so that is where I am learning the basics.

## Small preferences
I drink neither coffee nor tea. I'm a night person, not a morning person. I like casual and direct conversations, with a bit of humour.
