# About Minahil

I'm Minahil, a Computer Science student at the University of Management and Technology (UMT) in Lahore, Pakistan. I'm currently in my 7th semester of the BS Computer Science program.

My main interest is artificial intelligence. I'm exploring deep learning and neural networks, agentic AI and generative AI. Most of my projects so far connect to AI in some way, for example a skin disease detection app, a RAG chatbot, and running a small language model locally on my own laptop.

Besides studying, I work as a video editor and social media manager for a digital agency based in Dubai. I also run a small handcrafted decorative candle business called thecandlescent, where I make custom-packaged candles for occasions like birthdays and weddings.

I like casual, direct communication. When I explain academic topics out loud, I'm most comfortable mixing English and Roman Urdu.
